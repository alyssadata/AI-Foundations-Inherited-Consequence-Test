# Hypothesis

A prior constraint can materially affect a later trajectory after the constraint is no longer operating when evidence of the decision made under that constraint remains externally observable and reachable.

After a full reset, no task-specific model state, memory, prompt history, carried constraint, or hidden internal carrier is treated as surviving. A fresh activation may encounter the external public record of what occurred before reset.

If the fresh activation uses that record to preserve the already-selected path and does not reopen paths that were rejected and closed by the earlier decision, the prior constraint remains materially consequential to the later trajectory even though the constraint itself is no longer operating.

If fresh activations reliably reopen the closed paths despite verified access to the external record, the proposed inherited-consequence mechanism is not supported in that run set.

The test therefore asks whether externally preserved evidence of a real prior decision can constrain later path selection across reset without requiring anything inside the model to persist.
